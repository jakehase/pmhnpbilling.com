Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with PMHNP Billing.

What do you charge?

I charge 6% of collections—no setup fees, no monthly minimums. You only pay when you get paid. Prior authorizations are billed separately at $25 each.

What's included?

Full claims management, payment posting, denial management, insurance follow-up, and periodic revenue reviews. Everything you need to keep your billing running smoothly.

How does onboarding work?

After our initial conversation, you'll complete a short intake through the Client Portal. Once I have access to your EHR or billing system, I can typically start submitting claims within a few days.

Do you work with my EHR?

I work with most major EHR and practice management systems used by psychiatric providers. If you're unsure, just ask—I'm happy to check compatibility.

Ready to get started?

Let's discuss how I can help streamline your billing and get you paid faster.
