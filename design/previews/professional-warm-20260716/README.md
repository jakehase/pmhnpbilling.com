# PMHNP Billing — Professional + Warm homepage concept

Preview-only concept created from Jake's 2026-07-16 direction: retain professional trust while making the homepage warmer, more personal, and less like a large healthcare SaaS company.

## Design decisions

- Replaces the dark dashboard-style hero with an editorial note from Jake.
- Tones bright blue down in favor of deep teal, muted terracotta, sage, cream, and warm paper.
- Adds direct personal positioning without becoming playful or "cute."
- Replaces repeated boxed cards with open service rows and editorial spacing.
- Uses strong operational proof and a structured process to preserve credibility.
- Preserves the current production motion language: 4.5% logo hover/focus growth, 7.2-second logo breathing, 8.5/10-second hero accent breathing, scroll-triggered reveals, subtle scroll parallax, pointer tilt, button/service hover movement, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` shutdown.

## Boundary

This is a standalone mockup. It is not wired into production, published, or intended to change current SEO/form behavior until explicitly approved.
