CPT Code 90791 vs 90792: PMHNP Billing Guide (2026)

A direct guide to psychiatric diagnostic evaluations, time and documentation questions, E/M selection, and psychotherapy add-ons 90833, 90836, and 90838.

By PMHNP Billing editorial team · Published March 1, 2026 · 12 min read · Updated July 17, 2026

CPT code 90791 is used for a psychiatric diagnostic evaluation without medical services. CPT code 90792 is used for a psychiatric diagnostic evaluation with medical services. For PMHNP practices, the difference turns on the work performed and documented—not the clinician's title, prescribing authority, or visit length alone.

This national guide gives billing and clinical teams a practical framework to choose the code path, separate diagnostic and follow-up services, and reduce avoidable rework. Practices that want help applying these controls can review our psychiatric billing services; medication workflows that trigger utilization controls are covered in the prior authorization guide for PMHNPs.

Quick answer: Use 90791 when the diagnostic evaluation does not include medical services. Use 90792 when medical services are part of the diagnostic evaluation and the record supports them. For later medication-management visits, an outpatient E/M code may be more appropriate. Psychotherapy add-ons 90833, 90836, and 90838 attach to an E/M code—not to 90791 or 90792.

What is CPT code 90791?

CPT code 90791 represents a psychiatric diagnostic evaluation without medical services. The record should support the diagnostic work appropriate to the encounter, such as the presenting problem, relevant psychiatric and medical history, mental-status findings, risk assessment, diagnostic formulation, and treatment recommendations.

Is CPT code 90791 time based?

No psychotherapy minute range determines 90791. Code selection is based on the diagnostic service performed, while payer rules may still require actual time, limit frequency, or define when another evaluation is medically necessary. Do not select 90791 simply because an intake lasted a particular number of minutes.

Who can bill 90791?

A qualified clinician may report 90791 when the service is within the clinician's state scope of practice and the payer recognizes and credentials that clinician type for the code. PMHNPs should verify payer policy and contract terms; holding prescriptive authority does not automatically make every diagnostic encounter 90792.

90791 and 90792 documentation checklist

Use 90791 when:

  • The encounter is a psychiatric diagnostic evaluation without a medical-service component.
  • Documentation centers on psychiatric history, symptom profile, risk, diagnosis, and care planning.
  • The chart does not support medical assessment or management as part of the diagnostic service.

Use 90792 when:

  • The psychiatric diagnostic evaluation includes medical services relevant to diagnosis/treatment.
  • The note supports the medical assessment or management content expected by payer policy.
  • The encounter clearly captures medical complexity and treatment implications, not just psych history.
Compliance note: Do not choose 90792 merely because the rendering professional can prescribe medication. The medical service must be performed, medically necessary, and documented. Code pairing, frequency, and clinician eligibility can vary by payer and contract, so validate current CPT guidance and the policy effective on the date of service.

E/M + psychotherapy add-on pairing: where teams lose revenue

Codes 90833, 90836, and 90838 are psychotherapy add-ons reported with an appropriate E/M service. They are not standalone codes and should not be attached to 90791 or 90792. For Medicare guidance, the psychotherapy time excludes E/M time and the two services should be significant and separately identifiable.

Add-on code Psychotherapy time Report with
90833 16–37 minutes An appropriate E/M service
90836 38–52 minutes An appropriate E/M service
90838 53 minutes or more An appropriate E/M service

Document psychotherapy start and stop times or total psychotherapy time, the intervention, treatment goal, and patient response. Do not count E/M work toward psychotherapy minutes, and do not report a psychotherapy service shorter than 16 minutes under these code families.

Same-day psychiatric coding QA checklist

  • Confirm visit intent first: diagnostic evaluation vs ongoing management.
  • Validate whether medical services were actually performed/documented for initial evaluations.
  • For combined encounters, ensure E/M and psychotherapy sections are clearly distinguishable.
  • Check payer rules for code pairing, telehealth modifiers, and place-of-service edits.
  • Verify diagnosis pointers and problem linkage are consistent across note and claim.
  • Run pre-submit denial edits by payer before claim release.

Frequently asked questions about 90791 and 90792

What is CPT code 90791?

90791 is used for a psychiatric diagnostic evaluation without medical services. The chart should support the diagnostic assessment and resulting clinical formulation or recommendations.

What is the difference between 90791 and 90792?

90791 does not include medical services; 90792 does. The correct choice follows the work actually performed and documented, the clinician's scope, and payer policy.

Is CPT code 90791 time based?

90791 is not selected from a psychotherapy minute range. Payers may still impose documentation or frequency rules, so the note should support the complete, medically necessary diagnostic service.

Who can bill CPT code 90791?

A clinician whose license, scope, payer enrollment, and contract allow the service may bill 90791. Verify the payer's recognized provider types rather than relying on professional title alone.

Can 90833, 90836, or 90838 be added to 90791 or 90792?

No. Those psychotherapy codes are add-ons to an appropriate E/M service, not to 90791 or 90792. Confirm current payer edits before filing.

When would a PMHNP use 90792 instead of 99204?

CMS guidance says a psychiatric diagnostic evaluation with medical assessment may be reported with 90792 or an E/M code. The choice should reflect the service, documentation, payer rules, and applicable E/M requirements; do not report both for the same work merely because both appear plausible.

Can psychotherapy add-ons be billed at every follow-up?

Only when a distinct, medically necessary psychotherapy service is actually performed, the psychotherapy time supports the selected add-on, the E/M service is separately identifiable, and payer rules allow the combination.
