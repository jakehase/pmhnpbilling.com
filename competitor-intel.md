## Executive Summary

This report analyzes 5 key competitors in the PMHNP (Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner) and psychiatric practice billing services market. The medical billing industry for mental health providers is fragmented, with most competitors being generalist billing companies that offer psychiatric/mental health as one of many specialties. This presents a significant opportunity for a specialized PMHNP-focused billing service.

## Market Insights

### Pricing Models in the Industry:
1. **Percentage of collections** (most common): 4-8% typical
2. **Per claim/transaction**: $3-7 per claim
3. **Monthly flat fee**: Varies by provider count
4. **FTE (Full-Time Equivalent)**: Dedicated staff model
5. **Hybrid models**: Combination of base + percentage

### Common Industry Claims:
- 95-99% clean claim rates
- 10-30% increase in collections
- 15-20% reduction in cost-to-collect
- 20-40% faster payments

## GAPS & OPPORTUNITIES FOR JAKE

### 1. **PMHNP-Specific Specialization**
- **Gap:** No competitor focuses exclusively on PMHNPs
- **Opportunity:** Position as "The PMHNP Billing Expert" - understand unique coding (90834, 90837, 90791, 90847, E/M codes with psychotherapy add-ons), prior auth challenges for meds, and split billing complexities

### 2. **Transparent Pricing**
- **Gap:** None of the competitors show public pricing
- **Opportunity:** Publish clear pricing (e.g., "6% of collected revenue" or "$5 per claim") to build trust and reduce friction

### 3. **Small Practice Focus**
- **Gap:** Most competitors target larger practices or health systems
- **Opportunity:** Focus on solo PMHNPs and small group practices (1-5 providers) who are underserved

### 4. **PMHNP Education & Support**
- **Gap:** Competitors focus on technology/AI, not provider education
- **Opportunity:** Offer free coding education, monthly PMHNP billing webinars, and documentation templates specific to psychiatric care

### 5. **Medication Management Billing Expertise**
- **Gap:** Generic billing companies don't understand med management billing nuances
- **Opportunity:** Expertise in 99213-99215 with 90833/90836 add-ons, and documenting medical necessity for pharmacologic management

### 6. **Prior Authorization Services**
- **Gap:** Most billing companies don't handle prior auth for psychiatric medications
- **Opportunity:** Offer prior authorization support as add-on service (huge pain point for PMHNPs)

### 7. **Credentialing & Contracting**
- **Gap:** Limited focus on new PMHNP practice setup
- **Opportunity:** Full-service credentialing with insurance panels + initial contracting support

1. Own the PMHNP niche
