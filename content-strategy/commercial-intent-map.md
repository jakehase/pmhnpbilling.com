# Commercial search intent map

Updated: 2026-07-09

This map prevents keyword cannibalization and keeps every conversion CTA pointed at one primary service owner.

| Primary intent | Owning page | Supporting pages/content | Conversion offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| PMHNP billing services | `/services/pmhnp-billing-services.html` | Homepage and PMHNP operational articles | Full-service billing at 5% of collections; no setup fee or monthly minimum |
| Psychiatric billing services | `/services/psychiatric-medical-billing-services.html` | Psychiatric coding and denial articles | Psychiatric claims and RCM support |
| Mental health billing services | `/services/mental-health-medical-billing-services.html` | Behavioral-health payer and parity articles | Mental-health billing and payer workflow support |

| Prior authorization support | `/services/prior-authorization-support.html` | PA, renewal and medical-necessity content | $25 per completed authorization |

## Rules

- Do not create another service page for an intent already owned above.
- New articles support an owner with one contextual service link and one attributed contact link.
- Homepage owns the broad brand/category proposition; service pages own non-branded commercial queries.
- Search Console changes should retarget an existing owner before adding a new page.
- Article CTAs must carry `lead_source=article` and a topic-specific `lead_topic` value.
