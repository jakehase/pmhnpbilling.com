Site standards

Privacy notice

What the public PMHNP Billing website may collect, how information is used, and why visitors should not submit protected health information.

Last updated August 11, 2026

Who we are

PMHNP Billing LLC is an Illinois limited liability company that operates PMHNPBilling.com. “PMHNP Billing,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to PMHNP Billing LLC.

Information you choose to send

If you use the contact form, the site receives the information you enter so PMHNP Billing can review and respond to the inquiry. The public form is processed through Formspree. Do not submit patient names, medical records, member IDs, claim documents, or other protected health information through the public website.

Technical and service data

The site is delivered through hosting and security providers that may process ordinary technical data such as IP address, browser type, request time, referring page, and security events. The site also loads web fonts and may use Cloudflare's site-performance services.

For contact-form operations, the site may attach a random lead ID, submission time, prospect/test classification, attribution version, service topic, source or campaign tags, first landing path, and a referrer origin/path with query and fragment removed. Temporary attribution and successful-submit state may be stored in the browser's session storage and is cleared when the browser session ends or after the success record is consumed.

When Google Analytics is enabled, it is configured without advertising personalization or Google Signals and may collect page, device, referral, and conversion-event data. Analytics events do not intentionally include the contents of name, email, phone, practice, or message fields. A lead event is emitted only after the form service accepts a prospect submission; controlled tests use a separate event. These providers handle data under their own terms and privacy practices.

How information is used

  • to respond to inquiries and evaluate requested services;
  • to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website;
  • to prevent abuse or comply with applicable obligations;
  • to maintain business records related to a request.

PMHNP Billing does not use the public site to create patient records or provide clinical care.

Sharing and retention

Information may be processed by service providers needed to host, secure, and operate the site or contact workflow. It may also be disclosed when required to protect the site, enforce rights, or comply with law. Inquiry information is retained only as reasonably needed for the request and ordinary business records.

Your choices

You can browse without using the contact form. If you want to ask about information previously submitted, use the contact form without including patient information. Privacy practices may be updated when site services change; the update date on this page shows the current version.
