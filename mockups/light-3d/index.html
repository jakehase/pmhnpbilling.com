Safety and credibility should be visually prominent

For PMHNP Billing, trust is part of the product. The site should say the boundaries clearly and early.

Explicit trust boundary

PMHNP Claim Guard is positioned as risk visibility and recovery operations support — not an autonomous billing robot.

  • No automatic claim submission. Nothing here should imply background payer actions.
  • No direct payer changes. Recommendations remain drafts until a person reviews them.
  • Human review stays in the loop. That is a strength, not a disclaimer buried in the footer.

Why this design choice works

The light 3D treatment elevates the brand without making the product feel toy-like. It says “modern, premium, organized” without saying “risky, experimental, flashy.”
