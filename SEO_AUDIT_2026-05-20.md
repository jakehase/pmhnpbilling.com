# PMHNPBilling.com SEO Audit — 2026-05-20

## Scope

Audited the live site at `https://pmhnpbilling.com/` and the local source repo at `/root/clawd/pmhnpbilling-site`.

This audit covers indexability, sitemap/robots alignment, canonicalization, on-page metadata, headings, structured data, internal links, service-page content depth, observed search presence, and remaining deployment/external-search-console actions.

## Current search visibility observed

Observed through live search checks, not Google Search Console data:

- `site:pmhnpbilling.com pmhnp billing` returns indexed PMHNP Billing pages, including the blog, Illinois telehealth guide, homepage, and prior authorization service page.
- `PMHNP billing services` returned `https://pmhnpbilling.com/` at the top of observed results.
- `Illinois telehealth billing PMHNPs 2026` returned the PMHNP Billing Illinois telehealth guide at the top of observed results.
- `pmhnp billing services chicago` returned the PMHNP Billing blog in observed results, but not the primary service page in the top observed results.
- Broader/non-brand terms such as `psychiatric billing services PMHNP`, `PMHNP credentialing services Illinois`, and `PMHNP denial management appeals billing` showed competitors or informational pages ahead of PMHNP Billing in the top observed results.

Interpretation: the site is indexed and visible for exact PMHNP-focused and long-tail content, but broader service-intent rankings need stronger service landing pages, authority, and external signals.

## Live technical findings

### Good

- Live homepage returns `200` over HTTPS.
- `https://www.pmhnpbilling.com/` redirects to apex `https://pmhnpbilling.com/`.
- Live sitemap is reachable at `https://pmhnpbilling.com/sitemap.xml`.
- Live robots file allows search crawling and points to the sitemap.
- Live sitemap URLs tested returned `200`.
- App URLs carry `noindex` meta tags.

### Needs attention

1. **HTTP is not redirecting to HTTPS live**
   - `http://pmhnpbilling.com/` returned `200 OK` instead of `301/308` to HTTPS.
   - This is the largest remaining technical SEO/canonical issue.
   - Repo already has the Cloudflare handoff ruleset at `edge-handoff/cloudflare-https-redirect-ruleset.json`; it needs to be applied in Cloudflare.

2. **Robots file had duplicated Cloudflare-managed content**
   - Live robots showed Cloudflare managed content plus a duplicate copy from the repo.
   - Fixed locally by simplifying `robots.txt` and leaving Cloudflare to inject its own managed block.

3. **Sitemap listed `/app/` URLs while robots blocked `/app/`**
   - This sent conflicting signals: submitted in sitemap, blocked from crawling, and also noindexed in-page.
   - Fixed locally by removing `/app/` and `/app/intake.html` from `sitemap.xml`.
   - Fixed locally by allowing app crawl in `robots.txt` so crawlers can see each app page’s `noindex,nofollow,noarchive` meta if those URLs are discovered.

The local fixes improve the technical/on-page foundation, but actual ranking depends on crawl/index state, backlinks, authority, search intent, competition, and engagement signals.
