PMHNP Billing AI Agent (Pilot)

This page covers our optional AI Agent pilot for office workflow support. It is separate from our core billing services so you can review it clearly and decide if it is a fit for your team.

Ask about the pilot Back to main billing services

What this pilot is for

The AI Agent is designed to support day-to-day billing operations in a practical, office-friendly way:

  • Organize daily billing priorities and follow-up tasks
  • Help teams keep claim notes and action items in one place
  • Surface items that may need human attention sooner

Trust and safety: This is a pilot support tool. Human review is required before decisions or claim actions. It does not replace professional billing judgment, payer policy review, or compliance processes.

1) Discovery and fit

We start with a brief conversation about your workflow, payer mix, and team needs so expectations are clear before rollout.

2) Guided setup

Setup is completed with your office in mind. We keep the process simple and document who reviews what.

3) Pilot period

Your team uses the AI Agent in a controlled pilot window with manual checks and practical feedback loops.

4) Review and next steps

At the end of pilot, we review what helped, what did not, and whether to continue, pause, or refine.

What it does not promise

  • No guarantee of automatic claim approval or reimbursement outcomes
  • No "set it and forget it" billing replacement
  • No removal of staff responsibility for final review and compliance

Questions are welcome. If you want to explore whether this pilot is appropriate for your office, reach out through the Client Portal and we can talk through your workflow.
