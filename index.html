About

How I work

I started PMHNP Billing to provide psychiatric providers with reliable, attentive billing support across the United States. Many billing services treat you as one of hundreds of accounts. I take a different approach.

When you work with me, you get direct communication and consistent follow-through. I understand the specific requirements of psychiatric billing—from complex authorization processes to mental health parity regulations—and handle them with care.

My goal is straightforward: accurate claims, timely submissions, and fewer headaches for you.

FAQ

Common questions

What do you charge?

I charge 5% of collections—no setup fees, no monthly minimums. You only pay when you get paid. Prior authorizations are billed separately at $25 each.

What's included?

Full claims management, payment posting, denial management, insurance follow-up, and periodic revenue reviews. Everything you need to keep your billing running smoothly.

How does onboarding work?

After we confirm fit and sign the service agreement and Business Associate Agreement, access is set up through the practice's approved EHR, practice-management system, clearinghouse, or payer portal. Do not upload or email patient rosters, claims, appointment exports, or other patient information through this public website.

Do you work with my EHR?

I work with most major EHR and practice management systems used by psychiatric providers. If you're unsure, just ask—I'm happy to check compatibility.
