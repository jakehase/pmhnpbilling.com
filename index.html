About

Who runs PMHNP Billing

Founder and direct point of contact

Jacob “Jake” Hasenkamp

PMHNP Billing was founded in 2025, is based in Chicago, and supports PMHNP and psychiatric practices nationwide.

Jake works directly with practices on claim readiness, submission, payment posting, payer follow-up, denials, authorizations, and enrollment dependencies. Work is not handed off to a call center or an anonymous ticket queue.

  • Direct ownership: one accountable contact follows work from setup through resolution.
  • Source-grounded guidance: payer and regulatory statements are checked against current primary sources and the applicable product.
  • Clear boundaries: billing support does not replace clinical judgment, legal advice, or the provider’s responsibility for documented services and code selection.

FAQ

Common questions

What do you charge?

I charge 5% of collections—no setup fees, no monthly minimums. You only pay when you get paid. Prior authorizations are billed separately at $25 each.

What's included?

Full claims management, payment posting, denial management, insurance follow-up, and periodic revenue reviews. Everything you need to keep your billing running smoothly.

How does onboarding work?

After we confirm fit and sign the service agreement and Business Associate Agreement, access is set up through the practice's approved EHR, practice-management system, clearinghouse, or payer portal. Do not upload or email patient rosters, claims, appointment exports, or other patient information through this public website.

Do you work with my EHR?

I work with most major EHR and practice management systems used by psychiatric providers. If you're unsure, just ask—I'm happy to check compatibility.
