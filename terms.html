Website terms

Plain-language terms for using PMHNPBilling.com and its educational billing and revenue-cycle resources.

Last updated July 9, 2026

Educational website

PMHNPBilling.com provides general educational and operational information. It does not provide legal, medical, tax, compliance, or coding advice. Using the site or sending a contact inquiry does not create a client, clinician-patient, attorney-client, or other professional relationship.

Verify current requirements

Payer policies, code sets, laws, contracts, benefits, enrollment status, and claim instructions can change and can vary by product. You are responsible for verifying the source that applies to the provider, member, service, setting, and date. Do not alter clinical or billing facts merely to obtain payment.

No guarantee of outcome

The site does not guarantee claim payment, authorization approval, appeal success, reimbursement amount, compliance result, search ranking, revenue improvement, or uninterrupted access. Examples and checklists are starting points, not promises.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the site, attempt unauthorized access, interfere with operation, submit unlawful material, or place protected health information in a public form. Content may be referenced for internal educational use, but site text, branding, and design may not be republished as another service's work without permission.

Third-party services and links

The site links to government, payer, standards, and service-provider websites. PMHNP Billing does not control their availability, content, or privacy practices. A link is provided for convenience or verification and is not an endorsement.

Changes and questions

These terms may be updated as the site changes. Continued use after an update means the current terms apply to that use. Questions can be sent through the contact form.
