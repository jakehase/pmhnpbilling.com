What the percentage supports

Full-service billing covers the recurring revenue-cycle loop.

Claims and rejections

Prepare and submit claims from provider-approved encounters, resolve clearinghouse edits, and follow accepted claims into payer processing.

Posting and reconciliation

Post verified insurance and patient payments, adjustments, and responsibility; reconcile remits, deposits, takebacks, and variances.

Payer follow-up and denials

Work aging claims and genuine exceptions through correction, reconsideration, appeal, or provider clarification as appropriate.

Operational reporting

Separate routine activity from decisions and exceptions that need practice attention, with claim, payment, denial, and A/R visibility.

The exact scope is confirmed during the fit review and written agreement. Provider clinical documentation, diagnosis selection, and code approval remain provider responsibilities.

Separately scoped

Not every project belongs inside the percentage.

Prior authorizations

Prior-authorization support is separately priced at $25 per completed authorization where that service is selected.

Credentialing and enrollment

Credentialing, contracting, enrollment, roster, EDI, ERA, and EFT projects may require a separate scope.

Special projects

Legacy A/R, unusual transition work, or another stand-alone request is defined before work begins rather than silently added to the recurring fee.

Frequently asked questions

Pricing questions before a fit review.

Is the 5% fee based on charges or collections?

The published full-service billing fee is based on actual collections rather than submitted charges. The signed service agreement and reconciliation records control the final invoice calculation.

Does the estimate include insurance and patient payments?

The calculator accepts both to illustrate total collections. The written agreement defines the final fee base, transition cutoff, exclusions, and reconciliation method for an actual engagement.

Is there a setup fee or monthly minimum?

No. The published full-service billing offer has no setup fee and no monthly minimum.

Are prior authorizations included in the 5% estimate?

No. Prior-authorization support is separately priced at $25 per completed authorization where that service is selected. Credentialing, enrollment, and other stand-alone projects are also scoped separately.

Does paying a percentage guarantee collections?

No. PMHNP Billing manages the agreed billing operations, but coverage, adjudication, timing, recoupment, and payment decisions remain payer-controlled.

Does this calculator store information?

No. The arithmetic runs in your browser and does not submit the entered values. Do not enter patient information, claim details, credentials, or other protected health information.
