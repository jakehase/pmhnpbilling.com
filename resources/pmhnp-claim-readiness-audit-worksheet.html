1 Enrollment and payer route

  • Written participation and effective-date evidence is available for the applicable product.
  • Billing and rendering NPI/TIN configuration matches the payer enrollment record.
  • Taxonomy, service location, reassignment, and group roster are current.
  • The correct medical or behavioral-health administrator and payer ID are identified.
  • ERA/EFT enrollment status and remittance destination are confirmed.
  • Any unresolved enrollment dependency has an owner and follow-up date.

2 Eligibility, benefits, and authorization

  • Eligibility was checked for the date of service and exact product—not only the carrier name.
  • Behavioral-health benefits, carve-out routing, and network status were confirmed.
  • Referral and prior-authorization requirements were checked for provider, service, and setting.
  • Approved dates, units or visits, servicing provider, and renewal trigger are tracked.
  • Telehealth coverage, modality, patient location, POS, and modifier rules were verified.
  • The source, effective date, and verification date for the rule are recorded outside this sheet.

3 Documentation and service integrity

  • The signed record supports the service, medical necessity, diagnosis, and selected code.
  • Time-based services include the required time evidence under the applicable rule.
  • Psychotherapy add-on time, when used, is distinct from E/M work and documented separately.
  • Telehealth details that affect billing are supported by the record.
  • The rendering clinician—not an automated default—made the service and code selection.
  • Missing signatures, incomplete notes, or documentation questions are held before release.

4 Claim construction and release

  • Patient and subscriber data match the eligibility response in the billing system.
  • Billing, rendering, referring, and supervising fields are populated only when applicable.
  • Code, diagnosis pointer, units, POS, modifier, and authorization fields match the source record.
  • Duplicate-claim risk and any prior submission are checked before resubmission.
  • Practice and payer edits pass, with warnings resolved rather than automatically overridden.
  • The released claim retains a submission date and clearinghouse trace outside this worksheet.

5 First response, payment, and exceptions

  • 277CA or equivalent acceptance is confirmed; “sent” is not treated as payer acceptance.
  • Rejections are assigned for correction before the timely-filing window is at risk.
  • Payer status, control number, and follow-up date are retained in the billing system.
  • ERA/EOB posting is reconciled to allowed amount, adjustments, and patient responsibility.
  • Denials and underpayments have a root cause, next action, owner, and deadline.
  • Repeated exceptions are fed back into enrollment, intake, authorization, or documentation controls.
