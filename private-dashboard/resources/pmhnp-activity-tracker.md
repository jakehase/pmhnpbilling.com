## CRM Integration Notes

### Prospect Status Definitions
- **New**: Identified but no outreach yet
- **Research**: Gathering contact information
- **Connected**: LinkedIn connection accepted
- **Engaged**: Initial conversation started
- **Follow-up**: In follow-up sequence
- **Meeting Scheduled**: Call/meeting arranged
- **Proposal Sent**: Formal proposal delivered
- **Negotiation**: Terms being discussed
- **Client**: Successfully converted
- **Closed-Lost**: Not interested
- **Closed-Won**: Became client

### Next Action Categories
- **Research**: Need more information
- **Connect**: Send connection request
- **Follow-up**: Send follow-up message
- **Call**: Make phone contact
- **Email**: Send email outreach
- **Meet**: Attend scheduled meeting
- **Proposal**: Prepare/send proposal
- **Negotiate**: Discuss terms
- **Close**: Finalize agreement

### Priority Scoring System
**Demographics (1-3 points)**
- Location in target area: 1-3 points
- Solo/small practice: 1-3 points
- Active LinkedIn presence: 1-2 points

**Behavioral (1-4 points)**
- Posts about billing challenges: 1-4 points
- Engages with billing content: 1-3 points
- Recent practice startup: 1-2 points

**Firmographic (1-3 points)**
- Practice size (1-5 providers): 1-3 points
- Geographic desirability: 1-2 points
- Specialization match: 1-2 points

**Total Possible**: 10 points
**High Priority**: 8-10 points
**Medium Priority**: 5-7 points
**Low Priority**: 1-4 points
