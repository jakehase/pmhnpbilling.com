## Email Templates for Specific Scenarios

### High-Interest Prospect (asks lots of questions)
- Send more detailed case studies
- Offer references from current clients
- Provide ROI calculator
- Suggest trial period

### Price-Sensitive Prospect
- Emphasize ROI and typical payback period
- Offer payment plans
- Suggest starting with basic service level
- Provide guarantee terms

### Skeptical Prospect
- Share more client testimonials
- Offer references they can contact
- Provide detailed credentials/experience
- Suggest starting with small project

### Busy Prospect (hard to reach)
- Keep emails very brief
- Offer multiple contact options (text, email, phone)
- Send calendar links for easy scheduling
- Provide clear, simple next steps
