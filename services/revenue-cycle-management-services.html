Revenue Cycle Management Services for Small Practices

Revenue cycle management support that connects claim readiness, submission, payer response, payment posting, denial recovery, and A/R reporting into one practical workflow.

What this includes

  • Front-end claim readiness checks
  • Clean-claim submission and payer response tracking
  • ERA/EOB posting and variance review
  • Denial root-cause tracking and rework priorities
  • A/R follow-up queues with owner and next-action dates

Who this is for

Best for practices that want billing to become measurable and repeatable: fewer vague aging buckets, more clear next actions, and better visibility into where revenue is getting delayed.

Pricing note: billing work is currently 5% of collections, with prior authorizations billed separately at $25 each.

RCM is the operating system behind billing

Revenue cycle management is the full path from patient and payer details to usable cash. For small practices, the biggest gains often come from making the workflow visible: what is ready, what is submitted, what was rejected, what was denied, and what needs follow-up.

I focus on practical controls that reduce surprises: payer routing checks, authorization tracking, payment posting review, denial categories, and A/R follow-up rules.

Reporting that leads to action

A report is only useful if it points to the next decision. Revenue-cycle reporting should show clean-claim issues, denial mix, aging claims, payer delays, credentialing problems, and repeat intake or documentation issues.

For PMHNP and behavioral-health practices, this can help separate claim errors from payer-route problems, authorization gaps, telehealth modifier issues, and enrollment mismatches.

Revenue Cycle Management Services questions

What is included in revenue cycle management?

RCM includes claim readiness, claim submission, payer response tracking, payment posting, denial management, A/R follow-up, and reporting on issues that delay payment.

How is RCM different from basic billing?

Basic billing often focuses on claim submission. RCM looks at the whole cycle, including why claims fail, why payment is delayed, and what upstream workflow should change.

Can RCM help with psychiatric billing denials?

Yes. Psychiatric billing denials often repeat in patterns involving authorization, eligibility, provider enrollment, CPT/modifier combinations, telehealth rules, or payer routing. RCM makes those patterns visible.
