Credentialing support that connects to billing

Credentialing problems often show up later as claim denials: provider not on file, effective-date mismatches, taxonomy errors, group versus individual NPI confusion, ERA/EFT delays, and payer-roster gaps. This service focuses on the billing-side details that affect whether a newly credentialed PMHNP can actually get paid cleanly.

I help keep payer enrollment status, CAQH details, NPI/taxonomy alignment, contract/effective-date notes, and first-claim readiness organized so the first claims do not get stuck in avoidable payer setup issues.

First-claim readiness checklist

  • Confirm payer panel status, effective dates, payer IDs, billing/group setup, and rendering provider details before submission.
  • Track ERA/EFT setup, portal access, payer welcome letters, and any special behavioral health routing requirements.
  • Flag mismatches between CAQH, NPI registry, SimplePractice/EHR/practice-management setup, clearinghouse configuration, and payer rosters.

The result is a cleaner transition from “credentialed” to “actually billing and collecting,” especially for PMHNP practices launching insurance-based care.

Credentialing questions for PMHNP practices

Is this a replacement for a credentialing company?

Not always. Some practices already have a credentialing specialist. This support focuses on the billing-side handoff: making sure payer enrollment details, effective dates, group setup, CAQH information, and first-claim readiness do not fall through the cracks.

Why does credentialing affect denials?

Claims can deny when the payer roster does not match the submitted provider, tax ID, NPI, taxonomy, service location, or effective date. Catching those mismatches before submission can prevent weeks of avoidable follow-up.

When should this start?

Ideally before the first insurance claim goes out. That gives time to organize payer letters, portal access, ERA/EFT status, clearinghouse setup, and any plan-specific billing rules.
