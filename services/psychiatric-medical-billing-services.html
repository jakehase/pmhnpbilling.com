Psychiatric Medical Billing Services

Psychiatric billing support for PMHNPs, psychiatrists, and behavioral-health practices that need clean claims, authorization tracking, denial prevention, payment posting, and payer follow-up.

What this includes

  • Psychiatric claim-readiness checks before submission
  • Medication-management, evaluation, and psychotherapy add-on billing awareness
  • Prior authorization and renewal tracking support
  • Denial root-cause review for psychiatric claim patterns
  • A/R follow-up for payer delays, rejections, and underpayments

Who this is for

Best for psychiatric practices where claim accuracy depends on provider enrollment, CPT/modifier combinations, authorizations, telehealth requirements, behavioral-health routing, and documentation consistency.

Pricing note: billing work is currently 5% of collections, with prior authorizations billed separately at $25 each.

Psychiatric billing needs more than generic claim submission

Psychiatric practices face a mix of medical billing, behavioral-health payer rules, and clinical documentation realities. Claims can fail because of authorization gaps, place-of-service issues, telehealth modifier rules, payer carve-outs, provider enrollment mismatches, or documentation requests.

I help structure the billing workflow so those risks are checked before submission and tracked when payer responses come back.

A practical psychiatric revenue-cycle workflow

The workflow should connect scheduling and intake details to claim review, payer response, ERA/EOB posting, denial classification, appeal preparation, and reporting. That makes billing easier to manage and helps providers see which issues are operational versus policy-driven.

For PMHNP practices, this also means keeping credentialing, CAQH, NPI/taxonomy, ERA/EFT, and first-claim readiness tied to billing instead of treating enrollment as separate from cash flow.

Psychiatric Medical Billing Services questions

Who uses psychiatric medical billing services?

PMHNPs, psychiatrists, and behavioral-health practices use psychiatric medical billing services when they need help with claims, authorizations, denials, payment posting, payer follow-up, and revenue-cycle reporting.

What makes psychiatric billing different?

Psychiatric billing often involves medication-management visits, psychiatric evaluations, psychotherapy add-ons, telehealth rules, prior authorizations, behavioral-health carve-outs, and payer-specific claim routing.

Can you help with both billing and denials?

Yes. Billing support includes claim submission controls, payer follow-up, denial triage, root-cause tracking, and appeal-ready documentation when appropriate.
