Mental Health Medical Billing Services

Billing support for mental health practices that need help with behavioral-health payer rules, psychiatric CPT combinations, authorizations, telehealth billing, denials, and payment follow-up.

What this includes

  • Behavioral-health payer routing and carve-out checks
  • Psychiatric CPT/modifier claim-readiness review
  • Prior authorization tracking and renewal awareness
  • Telehealth POS/modifier and documentation controls
  • Denial and A/R follow-up for recurring behavioral-health issues

Who this is for

Best for mental health practices that want billing support from someone who understands why behavioral health claims fail differently than many general medical claims.

Pricing note: billing work is currently 5% of collections, with prior authorizations billed separately at $25 each.

Mental health billing has different failure points

Mental health claims often depend on behavioral-health carve-outs, authorization rules, place-of-service decisions, psychotherapy add-on coding, provider enrollment status, and payer-specific routing. A generic billing workflow can miss those details.

The goal is to turn those details into a predictable process before claims are submitted and before repeated denials turn into month-end surprises.

How behavioral-health practices reduce leakage

Revenue leakage often comes from small repeated problems: stale eligibility checks, missing authorization proof, wrong payer route, modifier mismatch, provider-not-on-file denials, or no-response claims that lack follow-up ownership.

A mental health billing workflow should keep those issues visible and feed them back into intake, scheduling, documentation, credentialing, and claim review.

Mental Health Medical Billing Services questions

What is mental health medical billing?

Mental health medical billing is revenue-cycle support for behavioral-health services, including psychiatric evaluations, medication management, psychotherapy add-ons, telehealth visits, authorizations, denials, and payer follow-up.

Why does behavioral health billing need specialized attention?

Behavioral-health claims often involve carve-outs, authorization rules, telehealth requirements, payer routing, and CPT/modifier combinations that generic billing workflows may not monitor closely enough.

Do you support PMHNP and psychiatric practices?

Yes. PMHNP, psychiatric, and behavioral-health practices are the core focus of the service.
