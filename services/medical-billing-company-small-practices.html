Medical Billing Company for Small Practices

Direct medical billing support for solo and small-group practices that want fewer billing headaches, cleaner claims, and practical follow-through without getting buried inside a large call-center model.

What this includes

  • Direct communication with the person managing the workflow
  • Claims, denials, payment posting, and payer follow-up in one process
  • Launch-stage support for payer setup and first-claim readiness
  • A/R review that turns aging claims into next actions
  • Simple reporting that practice owners can actually use

Who this is for

Best for practice owners who want a billing partner that feels close to the work: clear updates, documented next actions, and enough specialization to understand behavioral-health billing complexity.

Pricing note: billing work is currently 5% of collections, with prior authorizations billed separately at $25 each.

What small practices should expect from a billing company

Small practices need a billing company that can move quickly, explain what is happening, and keep ownership clear. The workflow should not disappear into a ticket queue where nobody can tell which claim needs what action.

A strong small-practice billing partner should connect front-end intake, payer enrollment, claim edits, ERA/EOB posting, denial reasons, and A/R follow-up instead of treating each piece as separate busywork.

Why direct communication matters

Billing issues are often time-sensitive. A claim may need corrected demographic data, authorization proof, payer-route confirmation, or provider enrollment evidence. Direct communication helps resolve those blockers before they age into lost revenue.

This approach is especially useful for PMHNP and psychiatric practices where one missing authorization, carve-out route, modifier, or credentialing detail can affect multiple claims.

Medical Billing Company for Small Practices questions

What makes PMHNP Billing different from a large billing company?

The model is direct and focused: you work with the person managing the billing workflow, and the process is built around small-practice revenue-cycle controls rather than call-center volume.

Can a small practice outsource billing before it is fully launched?

Yes. Launch-stage support can help organize payer setup, ERA/EFT details, EHR/practice-management access, first-claim checks, and early billing reports.

Is this only for PMHNP practices?

PMHNP and psychiatric billing are the primary focus, but the same small-practice billing workflow applies to other practices that need hands-on claim and A/R support.
