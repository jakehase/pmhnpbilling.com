Medical Billing Services for Small Practices

Medical billing support for practices that need clean claims, payment posting, denial follow-up, payer communication, and clear revenue-cycle visibility without building an internal billing department.

What this includes

  • Claim submission and claim-scrub workflow
  • Payment posting from ERA/EOB details
  • Denial tracking, rework, and appeal support
  • Insurance follow-up for aging or no-response claims
  • Monthly visibility into A/R, denial mix, and repeat billing issues

Who this is for

Best for small practices that want hands-on medical billing execution, especially psychiatric and behavioral-health practices where payer rules, authorizations, telehealth, and credentialing handoffs can create preventable revenue leakage.

Pricing note: billing work is currently 5% of collections, with prior authorizations billed separately at $25 each.

What strong medical billing support should control

Medical billing is not just submitting claims. A healthy workflow checks eligibility, demographics, payer routing, provider enrollment, authorization status, CPT/modifier combinations, and timely-filing risk before avoidable problems turn into A/R.

I organize billing around claim readiness, clean submission, payer response tracking, payment posting, and denial prevention. The goal is simple: every claim should have a status, next action, and owner.

Why small practices need a documented workflow

Small offices often lose revenue because claim work lives in inboxes, payer portals, and scattered notes. A documented billing process makes follow-up repeatable and helps the practice see whether denials are one-off issues or upstream workflow problems.

For psychiatric and PMHNP practices, this also means keeping authorizations, telehealth rules, credentialing status, and payer-specific behavioral health routing connected to the billing queue.

Medical Billing Services questions

What is included in medical billing services?

Core support includes claim submission, payment posting, payer follow-up, denial tracking, appeal preparation, A/R review, and reporting on recurring revenue-cycle issues.

Do you only work with psychiatric practices?

The service is strongest for PMHNP, psychiatric, and behavioral-health practices, but the same medical billing controls also help small practices that need clean claims and disciplined follow-up.

How is pricing handled?

PMHNP Billing currently charges 5% of collections for billing work, with no setup fees or monthly minimums. Prior authorizations are billed separately at $25 each.
